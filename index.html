Did You Know?

🌍

The world generates approximately 62 million metric tons of e-waste annually — equivalent to the weight of 5 Great Walls of China.

📱

For every 1 million smartphones recycled, 35,000 lbs of copper, 772 lbs of silver, and 75 lbs of gold can be recovered.

⚠️

Only 17.4% of global e-waste is properly recycled. The rest ends up in landfills, causing soil and water contamination.

💰

The raw materials in global e-waste are worth approximately \$91 billion annually — making it more valuable than most countries' GDP.

🔋

Improper disposal of lithium-ion batteries causes over 65% of waste facility fires. Proper recycling is critical for safety.
